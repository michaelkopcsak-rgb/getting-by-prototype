Getting By · prototype v6

Iteration 5 is a polish pass: dead ends now lead somewhere, the worry window works, and navigation, dates, names and accessibility are tidied up.

What changed in v6
  1. New visual system: Luminous Jade & Plum. Mint canvas, white cards, deep jade for actions, plum only for Get help and safety. Captions use #5E6F66 instead of the spec's #718279, which is too faint to read at small sizes (3.7:1).
  2. Type. Newsreader serif for headings and reflective text; Inter for everything you tap.
  3. Get help is a solid plum pill with a white lifebuoy on every screen. No orange or red anywhere.
  4. Floating frosted dock for the tabs; Right now stays in the centre.
  5. Right now choices are tiles; breathing and timers use jade rings.
What changed in v5
  1. Worry window works. On Today, "Park a worry" saves a few words for 5 pm. "Open the window early" shows each one with Let it go · Tomorrow · Act on it and the Three-Question Reset.
  2. No more dead ends in Right now. "Thoughts won't stop" now offers the Three-Question Reset, "hard on myself" offers the Self-Compassion Break, and "can't sleep" opens Write it down for the morning.
  3. Your names, everywhere. Get help, the check-in and Words for others use the person and counselor from your plan. Edit the plan and they update.
  4. Navigation. Tabs reset history, so Back never jumps somewhere odd. Screens open at the top. Today's date and the week view are correct.
  5. Accessibility. Visible focus rings, bigger chips (36px min), pressed states announced to screen readers, and reduced-motion respected everywhere.
  6. Content. The 5-4-3-2-1 steps read cleanly. The first plan level now uses the guide's wording: "Early signs".

Still placeholders: Weekly check-in, Bring to session, Settings, voice memo, and Read to me.

Profile and plan wording comes from Lifting the Fog, Sections 2 and 5. The three levels are a prototype idea, not from the guide yet. Try dark mode too.
